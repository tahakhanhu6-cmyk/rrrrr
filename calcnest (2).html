About CalcNest

CalcNest is a free, no-nonsense collection of everyday calculators. We built it because most calculator sites are cluttered with pop-ups and slow to load. Every tool here runs directly in your browser — nothing is uploaded, stored, or tracked beyond standard analytics.

Whether you're splitting a dinner bill, checking a loan payment, or working out your BMI, CalcNest aims to give you an answer in under ten seconds.
